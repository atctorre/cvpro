How to write a sales representative resume that sells itself

A sales resume without numbers is a contradiction. The sales manager reading it wants quota, attainment, pipeline and deal size in the first ten seconds; if those are missing, they assume you do not have them. In this field modesty is not a virtue — it is a competitive disadvantage.

What the sales manager looks for

  • Quota attainment. The central number. "Achieved 112% of annual quota" or "exceeded target 9 of 12 months". If you missed, show the improvement trend.
  • Type of sale. B2B or B2C, consultative or transactional, short or long cycle, inside or field. Each needs a different profile and the manager needs to place you.
  • Pipeline and prospecting. Accounts managed, accounts opened, how you sourced them. Generating new business is worth more than farming inherited accounts.
  • Tools. CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho), LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Outreach or similar, Excel for forecasting. Show you work with process, not memory.
  • Industry and deal size. SaaS, insurance, medical devices, retail, real estate. Changing industries is possible, but explain what transfers.

Mistakes that get a sales resume rejected

  • No numbers. "Responsible for sales in the Northeast" says nothing. "Northeast territory: 80 accounts, 25% year-over-year growth" is what gets read.
  • Confusing activities with results. Visiting clients is an activity. Closing 15 new accounts in a quarter is a result. Only the second earns interviews.
  • Hiding bad years with vague dates. Sales managers know there are down years. Show how you responded; unexplained gaps are what disqualify.
  • Salesperson adjectives. "Aggressive", "natural closer", "results-driven". Numbers speak for you; adjectives read as insecurity.
  • Omitting deal size. Selling 100 units at $10 is not the same as 10 at $10,000. Without average deal value, your numbers cannot be interpreted.

If you have never held a formal sales title

Almost everyone has sold something: retail floor, catalog or social-media sales, a family business, or convincing customers in a service role. Rewrite it with metrics: how much you sold, to how many customers, how often they came back. Customer service with cross-selling is sales. Selling on your own is sales — state volume and duration. Add courses in sales techniques, negotiation or CRM. In your summary, prove commercial drive with one concrete result, not the word "driven".

Frequently asked questions

What if I did not hit quota?

Show the trajectory: "From 70% to 95% attainment over three quarters". Sustained improvement is a selling point. Omitting numbers entirely is what fails.

Should I include social-media selling or a side business?

Yes, with honest figures: product, period, approximate volume, repeat customers. It shows initiative and closing ability.

Should I list commissions or income?

No. List quota, attainment and volume sold. Your earnings are private and do not help the hiring manager.

How do I switch industries?

Emphasize what transfers: sale type (B2B/B2C), cycle length, tools, speed of learning a new product. Explain the move in one line of the summary.

How long should it be?

One page for under ten years. Sales managers read fast; a tight page with numbers beats two pages of activities.
