Gifts for Him: How to Choose for a Man Who Works in an Office

Choose by how he works: a briefcase if he carries a laptop to the office, or a duffel if he travels. Then count back from the date you need the gift. Estimated delivery is 15 to 25 days from the order date, so order at least thirty days ahead.

This is a decision guide, not a ranked list. The items in Professional Essentials are fulfilled by an overseas supplier, so the order-by date matters more than it does for most gifts for him, and it is easy to overlook.

Start with how he works

Two questions settle most of the choice. Does he carry a laptop to work? Does he travel, for work or for weekends?

Order-by dates for a 15 to 25 day delivery

Estimated delivery is 15 to 25 days from the order date, to addresses in the United States. The rule: order at least thirty days before the date you need the gift. That is the 25-day end of the range plus five days of margin. An estimate is not a promise, and a date that only works at the short end of the range is a risk.

If the last date has already passed, choose a later occasion for the gift or buy it elsewhere.

If you are buying Thanksgiving gifts for men, October 27 is the date to beat. Father's Day is the third Sunday of June, so the same rule puts the order in May, and ordering by mid-May leaves a margin. For birthdays, anniversaries and graduations, count back thirty days from the date.

Gifts by recipient: dad, husband, son, coworkers and boss

Gifts for dad and gifts for husband. A briefcase if he carries a laptop, after you confirm its size, or a duffel if he travels. The 17-inch briefcase is listed as Casual and the 15-inch as Dress, which is a quick way to match the bag to his office. Because neither briefcase lists interior dimensions, the outside measurements are only a first check, so email us if his laptop is close to the limit.

Gifts for a son. Style is the guide. The listings give style names: Casual for the 17-inch briefcase, Dress for the 15-inch briefcase, Vintage for the vintage duffel and fashion for the travel duffel. Pick the name that matches how he dresses and what he carries.

Gifts for coworkers and gifts for boss. Keep it modest. Some workplaces set a limit on the value of a gift or discourage gifts to a manager, so read the policy before you buy. Do not present any item as a business accessory beyond what its listing says.

Gifts by occasion: Christmas, Father's Day, birthday, anniversary, graduation

Christmas. Christmas gifts for men carry the tightest dates. Order by November 25 to leave a margin; November 30 is the last date that fits even the 25-day end of the range. Caution: confirm the laptop size before you order a briefcase.

Father's Day. Count back thirty days from the third Sunday of June, which puts the order in May. A duffel suits a father who travels and a briefcase one who carries a laptop. Caution: the duffels are travel bags, so do not choose one for a father who needs to carry a laptop.

Birthday. For birthday gifts for men, count back thirty days from the date. Caution: the listings name colors only and do not describe tone or finish, so choose by name with that in mind.

Anniversary. For anniversary gifts for him, the vintage duffel is listed as "Style: Vintage" and "Function: Lockable". Caution: a lock is not listed as included, and no gift box or gift wrapping comes with any item.

Graduation. Graduation gifts for him may mark a first job, and a briefcase suits that. Graduation dates are fixed, so count back thirty days. Caution: confirm the laptop size first, because the briefcase listings do not give interior dimensions.

What is not included

No listing comes with a certificate for its material, and most weights are listed without a unit. The vintage duffel is listed as lockable but a lock is not listed as included, and no shoulder strap is listed for either briefcase.

What to confirm before you order

Five things, before you pay:

  1. The recipient's laptop size, if you are buying a briefcase.
  2. A delivery address in the United States, and that someone can accept the package.
  3. The date you need the gift, and the date thirty days before it.
  4. The color name, since the listings give names only. The 17-inch briefcase lists Coffee, Black and Brown, the 15-inch briefcase Dark Coffee and Brown, the travel duffel Black or Brown and the vintage duffel Black or Light Brown.
  5. A working email address, in case you need to reach us about the order. Ours is hola@getcvpro.com.
