Shipping, Sales and Refund Policy

Before you buy: all sales final. We do not accept returns or exchanges, and we do not refund a change of mind. Two exceptions:

  • Not delivered: a full refund if your order is not delivered by day 30 after your order date. Estimated delivery is 15 to 25 days.
  • Damaged or wrong item: a replacement or refund if you report it with photos within 7 days of delivery.

1. Delivery

  • Where items come from: items are fulfilled by overseas suppliers and shipped to addresses in the United States. The listings state Mainland China / CN / Guangdong as the origin. We do not keep stock in the United States.
  • Estimated delivery: 15 to 25 days from the date you place your order. Shipping is included in the price shown, and prices are in US dollars. Tracking is available.
  • An estimate: the range is an estimate, and we cannot promise a specific arrival date.
  • If we cannot ship on time: if we learn that we cannot ship your order within that time, we email you a revised date and the choice to wait or to cancel for a full refund.
  • Import costs: the price includes an allowance for U.S. import costs, and checkout adds no separate duty charge. If a carrier asks you to pay anything at delivery, email hola@getcvpro.com before you pay.

2. All sales final

We do not accept returns or exchanges, and we do not refund a change of mind. This applies to every item and every order. It includes ordering the wrong size or color, or finding that the item does not suit you, when the item matches its listing. Check the specifications, measurements and delivery estimate on the product page before you pay. Every specification on our pages is stated as the supplier's listing gives it; where the listing says nothing, the page says "not listed".

3. Exception 1: your order is not delivered

If your order is not delivered by day 30 after your order date, you can request a full refund of the amount you paid for the items that were not delivered.

  • Your order date is the date on your order confirmation email. Day 1 is the day after your order date. Day 30 is the 25-day end of our delivery estimate plus a five-day margin for carrier and customs delays.
  • You can request the refund from day 31. If the carrier's tracking shows the package as lost or returned to the sender, you can request it earlier.
  • If tracking shows the package as delivered but you did not receive it, email us within 7 days of the delivery scan. We check with the carrier and refund you in full if the package cannot be located.
  • You do not need to send photos for a package that was not delivered. We check the tracking.

4. Exception 2: your item is damaged, defective or wrong

If an item arrives damaged or defective, is not the item you ordered (wrong product, size or color), or is materially different from its listing on our product page, report it with photos within 7 days of the delivery date shown in the carrier's tracking. We will replace the item or refund the amount you paid for it, at your choice. A replacement depends on availability; if one is not available, we refund you. You do not need to send the item back.

Not covered: a change of mind; a size or fit you did not expect when the item matches its listing; natural variation in the material's grain or shade; normal wear; damage after delivery.

5. How to report a problem

  1. Email hola@getcvpro.com. Put your order number and the problem in the subject line, for example "Order 12345: damaged item" or "Order 12345: not delivered".
  2. Include your order number (it is in your confirmation email) and the name you used at checkout.
  3. For a damaged, defective or wrong item, attach at least two photos: the whole item, and a close-up of the damage or the difference. If you can, also attach a photo of the outer packaging and the shipping label, and a short description of the problem.
  4. We reply within 2 business days. We decide within 5 business days of receiving what we need. Business days are Monday to Friday.

6. Refunds and replacements

  • Refunds: a refund goes to your original payment method within 7 business days of our approval. Your bank or card issuer may need additional time to show it. We charge no fee, and refunds are not issued as store credit.
  • Amount: the refund covers the amount you paid for the affected items. Shipping is included in the price.
  • Replacements: we place the replacement order within 3 business days of our approval and email you the tracking number when it is available. A replacement ships from an overseas supplier, so the same 15 to 25 day estimate applies, counted from the date we approve it.

7. Warranty and your rights

We do not offer a separate warranty. This policy does not limit rights you have under law. Please write to us first so we can fix a problem quickly. You can ask for a written copy of this policy at any time at hola@getcvpro.com.
