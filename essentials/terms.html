We do not accept returns or exchanges, and we do not refund a change of mind. This applies to every item and every order. 3. Exception 1: your order is not delivered

If your order is not delivered by day 30 after your order date, you can request a full refund of the amount you paid for the items that were not delivered.

  • If tracking shows the package as delivered but you did not receive it, email us within 7 days of the delivery scan. We check with the carrier and refund you in full if the package cannot be located.

If an item arrives damaged or defective, is not the item you ordered (wrong product, size or color), or is materially different from its listing on our product page, report it with photos within 7 days of the delivery date shown in the carrier's tracking. We will replace the item or refund the amount you paid for it, at your choice. A replacement depends on availability; if one is not available, we refund you. You do not need to send the item back.

Not covered: a change of mind; a size or fit you did not expect when the item matches its listing; natural variation in the material's grain or shade; normal wear; damage after delivery.
